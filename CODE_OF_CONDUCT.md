# Code Of Conduct

Reflective's public community should be useful, professional, and safe for
developers evaluating or improving the product.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and respectful.
- Critique ideas, workflows, and docs without attacking people.
- Keep issues actionable and on-topic.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Assume public posts may be read by teams evaluating Reflective.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Posting private code, secrets, credentials, or sensitive company data.
- Spam, low-effort promotion, or unrelated requests.
- Repeatedly reopening closed topics without new information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation
when needed to protect the project and community.
